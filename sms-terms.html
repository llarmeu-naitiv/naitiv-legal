SMS Terms

Last updated: 24 November 2026

These terms cover text messages from Naitiv's interview code programme.

The programme. When you apply for a job with Naitiv and agree to receive text messages, we text you a one-time code to confirm it is you before each of your AI-led interviews. We send nothing else by text: no marketing and no promotions.

How you agree. You opt in by giving your mobile number and ticking the text message box on our application form. You don't have to: if you leave the box unticked, we send your codes by email instead.

How often. About 1 to 3 messages per interview round, only when you ask for a code on your interview page.

Cost. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Stopping messages. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from us. We will send one message to confirm, then no more. We will send any further codes by email. To start again, reply START. Texts to UK mobiles come from the sender name NAITIV, which cannot take replies: to stop those, email privacy@naitiv.com and we will email your codes instead.

Help. Reply HELP to any message, or email privacy@naitiv.com.

Carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy. Our Privacy Policy explains how we use your information. We do not share your mobile number, or your agreement to receive texts, with anyone for marketing.

Who we are. Naitiv Partners Inc, Denver, Colorado, United States, and its UK subsidiary, registered in England and Wales with company number 17467007.
