Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Naitiv collects and uses personal information when you apply for a job with us, take part in our interviews, receive text messages from us, or contact us. Naitiv is an AI-native company.

Who we are

"Naitiv", "we" and "us" means Naitiv Partners Inc, a company based in Denver, Colorado, United States, and its UK subsidiary, registered in England and Wales with company number 17467007. For job applications in the UK, the UK company is the controller of your personal information. For job applications in the United States, Naitiv Partners Inc is.

You can contact us about this policy or your information at privacy@naitiv.com.

What we collect

When you apply for a job: your name, email address, mobile number, where you are based, your LinkedIn profile if you give it, your right to work, notice period and the type of work you are looking for, your CV, anything else you choose to tell us, and how you heard about the role.

When you take part in our interviews: for our AI-led first and second interviews, a written transcript of the interview, the time it took, and whether you chose to talk or type. We do not keep a recording of your voice. For our third interview, the notes and scores of the Naitiv person you meet.

When we text you: your mobile number, the text messages we send you, and whether they were delivered.

When you contact us: your name, contact details and what you tell us.

We do not ask about your health, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation or other special categories of information. If you volunteer any of these during an interview, we remove that part of the transcript before anyone scores it.

How we use it

  • To consider your application, run our interviews, make a decision and tell you the outcome.
  • To confirm it is you before an interview, by sending a one-time code by text or email.
  • To arrange your interviews and any adjustment you ask for.
  • To keep a record of our recruitment decisions, so that we can answer questions about them and show they were fair.
  • To reply to you when you contact us.

How AI is used, and who decides

We use AI to help us, and a person always makes the decision. Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, reads your CV against what the role needs and drafts a summary for a person at Naitiv to check. Our AI interviewer, which runs on ElevenLabs, asks every candidate for a role the same questions. Claude then drafts scores from the written transcript, and a person at Naitiv scores your interview themselves before they see the draft. No decision about you is made by AI alone. You can ask for a person to review any result, and you can ask for an interview with a person instead of the AI interviewer at any time.

Our legal basis (UK and EU)

We use your information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract of employment with you, and for our legitimate interest in recruiting fairly and efficiently. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have checked that they do not override your rights.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the people at Naitiv involved in your application and with these service providers, who act on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes:

  • ServiceNow, which hosts our recruitment system.
  • Anthropic, which provides the Claude AI model. Anthropic does not use our data to train its models.
  • ElevenLabs, which runs our AI interviewer. It keeps interview transcripts for no more than 30 days and no audio.
  • Twilio, which sends our text messages.
  • Microsoft, which provides our email.

If an agency or a person referred you to us, we may tell them where your application has got to.

Text messages

We send text messages only to confirm it is you before an interview: a one-time code, about 1 to 3 messages per interview round. We send them only if you agreed to receive them when you applied, and you can stop them at any time by replying STOP, or for texts to UK mobiles by emailing privacy@naitiv.com. Our SMS Terms explain the programme in full.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers who send our messages for us.

International transfers

Some of our service providers process information in the United States and other countries. Where information about someone in the UK or EU is transferred outside those areas, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the UK-US Data Bridge and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified.

How long we keep it

If you are not offered a role, we keep your application, transcripts and scores for nine months after our decision, then delete them, unless you ask us to keep your details for future roles. If you join us, your application becomes part of your employee record. Text message logs are kept for the same period as your application.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, ask us to restrict its use, or ask for it to be moved to another organisation. You can ask for a person to review any decision that involved AI. To use any of these rights, email privacy@naitiv.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In the EU you can complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in the United States, your state may give you further rights, and you can use them the same way.

Security

We keep your information in access-controlled systems. Interview links are personal to you and expire, and we confirm it is you with a one-time code before an interview starts.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
